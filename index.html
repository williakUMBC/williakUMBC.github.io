William Paul Kelly

I am working with Dr. Konstantinos Kalpakis on developing a machine learning(ML) pipeline to detect severe medical conditions from electrocardiograms(ECGs), or heart monitor recordings. This work may later be useful for producing facsimile ECGs for training future models.

I am currently a PhD student at University of Maryland Baltimore County. In addition to my coursework and research work, I also work as a TA, holding office hours for project help in the operating systems class

Previously, I graduated Magna cum Laude from Clemson University before briefly working as a volunteer researcher at University of North Carolina Wilmington(UNCW). I presented my work with UNCW at the Southeastern Consortium for Computing Science in Colleges Conference in November of 2024, which began my interests in AI and ML.
